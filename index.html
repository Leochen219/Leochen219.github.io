Research Interests

My interests are multimodal representation learning, world models, and post-training. I study how training data distributions and learning objectives shape performance and generalization, and how targeted data construction, reinforcement learning, and distillation can address their limitations.

Research

Navigation World Models
Ongoing Research

Experiments evaluating a navigation world model found policy-like behavior favoring familiar navigation patterns over alternative actions. I developed an Isaac Sim data pipeline integrating NavDP, A* planning, and perturbation-based trajectory collection to support targeted fine-tuning. The research question concerns trajectory coverage, training distributions, and action-conditioned simulation fidelity.

VLM Post-Training
Ongoing Research

Exploring reinforcement learning and on-policy self-distillation for vision-language models, with a focus on multimodal reasoning and generalization. I study how post-training objectives and data shape behavior and how models use visual evidence.

Manuscript

ScaleGen: Scaling Up Perceptual Super-Resolution with Million-Scale Diffusion-Generated Images
Under review at IEEE Transactions on Multimedia
Third author

Uses large text-to-image models to construct a million-image synthetic corpus for compact diffusion-based super-resolution models, targeting competitive perceptual quality without large generators at inference. The work connects synthetic training data with performance and efficiency.
